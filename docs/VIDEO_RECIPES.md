# Video Recipes

Start with the closest spec. Change content and art direction, not engine rules.

## Explainer

Use: hook, problem, mechanism, proof, action. Use `explain`, timeline, bars,
and one mechanic. Keep each claim visual and factual.

## Product Launch

Use: tension, reveal, three benefits, proof, call to action. Use hero photo,
detail crops, a counter or card stack, and strong brand chrome.

## Documentary

Use: human observation, context, evidence, turn, invitation. Use restrained
type, photos, maps/timelines, quotes only when supplied, and slow motion.

## Data Story

Use: surprising number, comparison, cause, implication, action. Use one chart
per claim. Label units. Never animate numbers without source facts.

## Social Promo

Use: pattern break in the first second, one promise, two proof beats, direct
CTA. Use larger type, quicker transitions, and a ticker or progress bar.

## Default Direction

- Vertical: 1080x1920 at 30 fps.
- Review: `--scale 0.5`; final size only after still approval.
- Text: one short idea per screen. Display text should read in under two seconds.
- Layers: overlap by 0.5-1.2 seconds. Avoid a hard cut between every beat.
- Visuals: use supplied assets first; otherwise use deterministic shapes,
  diagrams, charts, and typography.
