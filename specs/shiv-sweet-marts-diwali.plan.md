# Shiv Sweet Marts — Diwali Sweet Boxes

## Direction
- Audience: Families in the local 5 km area
- Objective: Drive walk-ins this week for Diwali sweet boxes
- Format: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 18 seconds
- Style: Paper-cut collage
- Palette: #90c141, #00bdcc, deep ink #10211f
- Evidence: ₹199 for 6 pedhas; ₹299 for 3 barfis
- CTA: Visit Shop Now

## Voice script
1. Diwali ki mithaas ghar le jaana hai?
2. Shiv Sweet Marts ke sweet boxes dekhiye.
3. ₹199 mein 6 pedhe!
4. ₹299 mein 3 barfi!
5. Is week shop par aaiye. Visit Shop Now!

## Persistent continuity
- Full-duration ground layer with a paper-texture treatment created from native shapes.
- Persistent top brand strip and bottom ticker.
- Persistent progress bar with keyframes at each beat boundary.
- Beat layers overlap by 0.5 seconds; no hard scene-to-scene cuts.

## Contrast pairs
Approved text/background pairs:
- #90c141 on #10211f
- #00bdcc on #10211f
- #ffffff on #10211f

All text sits on solid deep-ink plates. Minimum display size: 64 px.

## Style lock
Paper-cut collage made from matte colored paper, crisp hand-cut edges, subtle layered-paper depth, soft contact shadows, restrained grain, bold simplified silhouettes, and a festive Indian Diwali palette using #90c141 and #00bdcc with deep ink accents. Keep every object isolated, centered, and separated from adjacent cells.

## Atlas plan
- `sheet-1.png`: 3×3 grid, key #FF00FF — pedha, barfi, diya, rangoli motif, marigold cluster, toran segment, puja thali, gift box, decorative paper burst.
- `sheet-2.png`: 3×2 grid, key #FF00FF — sweet-box lid, sweet-box base, pedha cluster (six), barfi trio, diya pair, marigold corner flourish.
- `hero-1.png`: standalone hero image — open Diwali sweet box with pedhas and barfis, paper-cut collage composition. No text or logo.

## Engine constraint
The current Custom Hyper capability manifest says image generation is not an engine capability. The assets must be generated outside the renderer and supplied as files. The renderer itself remains deterministic and offline.
